Actual Gas Stoves More Like an Old Apartment Super Quiet

The Specs

What you're getting

Address

3320 Powelton Ave. It's a bit of a walk from the center of campus.

Cost

About $4,310 per term

The Setup

3 and 4-person suites. Every single room layout is slightly different because it's a historic building.

Bike Storage

Down in the basement. Use it if you have a bike, street parking here is risky.

The Van R Vibe

Van R is easily the oldest building on the housing list, which means it doesn't feel like a standard, boring college dorm at all. It feels way more like a private apartment building. Because it has the smallest population of any first-year hall, the whole place stays pretty quiet and low-key throughout the term.

The Stoves: You get a private kitchenette with an actual gas range. If you hate dining hall food and want to cook real meals, this is the only place on campus where you can do it.
Top Floor Lounge: If you need to study without being bothered, the lounge on the very top floor has great views and is almost always completely empty.

The Odd Layouts

The suites average roughly 710 square feet, but because it’s an old historic property, almost every unit has a different shape. Your bedroom might have a weird alcove or a different wall angle than the room right next door. You get the basic Twin XL beds, desks, and small dressers, but fitting your clothes in can require some creativity.

The Resident Reality

Van R is great if you value your privacy, but the location is an adjustment. It’s located further out on Powelton, so walking to class in the winter sucks. It can also get a little socially isolating. Since everyone has their own kitchen and living space, people tend to stay locked inside their suites instead of hanging out in the hallways.

Practical Details

  • The Bathrooms: Every suite has its own private, all-gender full bathroom. You clean it yourself, but you never have to wait in line.
  • First Floor Ballroom: There's a random historic ballroom on the ground floor with a baby grand piano. People actually use it to practice.
  • Laundry: It's down in the basement level, which means carrying your heavy laundry baskets down several flights of stairs depending on your floor.

Just a student's perspective on the layout, not official university marketing copy.
