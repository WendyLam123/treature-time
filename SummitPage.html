The Specs

The big apartment tower

Address

3400 Lancaster Ave. Right on the corner of 34th, very close to Lancaster food spots and food trucks.

Cost

About $924 - $1,704 per month

The Setup

Fully furnished suites and apartments. There is a huge gym on the first floor and study areas on the upper levels.

Who Lives Here

Mostly sophomores dealing with the mandatory housing rule, some upperclassmen, and occasionally Penn Students. Summit is open to any student or faculty from Drexel or Penn.

The Summit Vibe

If you want your sophomore year to feel like you actually live in a city apartment instead of a dorm, the Summit is where most people go. It has an industrial style with exposed concrete ceilings, big windows, and lots of spots to hang out. Because it sits right on top of Urban Eatery, you don't even have to step outside into the winter cold to grab dinner or a coffee.

The Kitchens: If you get a full apartment layout, you get a real kitchen with a full-sized fridge, stove, and a dishwasher. It makes cooking for yourself way easier if you want to drop the campus meal plan.
The Gym & Lounge: The gym downstairs is huge, so you don't have to walk all the way to the DAC to workout. Also, the lounge on the top floor has some of the best views of the Philly skyline on campus.

Room Setup

They have everything from shared suites to 4-bedroom setups where you get your own private room. A big plus is that the rooms come with full-sized beds instead of those annoying, narrow twin XL mattresses you find in freshman dorms. You also get living room furniture like a couch and a TV stand, plus your own washer and dryer.

The Resident Reality

The Summit looks nice when you first see it, but dealing with management can be a huge headache and maintenance takes a while to fix things since it is run by ACC instead of Drexel. The walls are pretty thin, so if your neighbor turns on a speaker, you are going to hear it. Also, the elevators break down all the time, which is terrible if you live up on the 20th floor.

Practical Details

  • The Bathrooms: Depends on what you pay for. You either get a bathroom attached right to your private room, or you share one with a couple of roommates.
  • Eating: You are right above Urban Eatery, and it is a super short walk to all the food trucks parked along Market Street.
  • The Leases: Everyone signs an individual lease. That means if your roommate decides to move out or stops paying, it doesn't affect your rent at all.
  • Utilities: Internet, water, and trash are all included in your monthly base rent fee, so you don't have to worry about extra utility bills.
