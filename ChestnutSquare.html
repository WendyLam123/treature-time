The Specs

Super central apartments

Address

3200 Chestnut Street. It is basically in the middle of campus right on the main street.

Cost

About $989 - $1,744 per month

The Setup

A mix of mid-rise buildings and a high-rise tower. There are fast food places on the first floor and a courtyard in the middle.

Who Lives Here

Mostly sophomores doing their second-year housing requirement, and some upperclassmen.

The Chestnut Vibe

If you hate waking up early, Chestnut Square is probably the most convenient option on campus. It is built right on top of the shops on Chestnut Street, so places like Shake Shack, Bubblefish, and the campus bookstore are right under your room. It feels really connected to the rest of campus and the city, and there is always people walking around outside.

The Townhomes: They have these cool two-story townhome layouts. Having actual stairs inside a college apartment makes it feel a lot more like a real house and less like a cramped dorm room.
The Courtyard: The outdoor courtyard in the center of the complex is a really nice spot to sit, eat lunch, or do homework when it finally gets warm in the spring term.

Room Setup

You can get 1, 2, or 4-bedroom layouts. You can either pay more to have your own private bedroom or share a room with someone if you want to keep the monthly rent cheaper. They come fully furnished with beds, dressers, desks, and a kitchen. Plus, you get a washer and dryer right inside your unit so you don't have to carry laundry down a hall.

The Resident Reality

The location is amazing, but it can get really loud at night. Because you are right on Chestnut Street, you are going to hear city traffic, sirens, and loud people walking past late at night—definitely bring earplugs if you sleep lightly. The bedrooms in the shared floor plans feel pretty tight compared to places like the Summit, and the front desk staff can be way too strict about checking guests in.

Practical Details

  • The Bathrooms: Pretty standard, clean bathrooms. Depending on your layout, you either share with one roommate or split it among the whole apartment.
  • Security: You have to swipe your key-card at the main doors and walk past a security desk to get up to the rooms.
  • Social Space: There is a resident game room, a small theater room, and some study lounges spread across the building.
  • Co-op Stuff: Since it is an ACC building, they have specific leasing rules that make it a bit easier to handle your rent if your co-op job forces you to move away from Philly for six months.
