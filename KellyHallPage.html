Overview

Kelly Hall is the go-to choice for social butterflies. Following its massive 2023 renovation, the building is fresh and modern while keeping its reputation as one of the easiest places to meet people.

The star of the show is the "Kelly Commons" addition, which provides a massive amount of study and social space on the lower levels.

Building Layout

Kelly features a unique "neighborhood" plan. A central spiral staircase connects two floors at a time, leading into a shared double-height lounge. This design encourages everyone to leave their rooms and hang out in the common areas.

Rooms

Rooms are traditional doubles with vinyl tile flooring. If you can snag a corner unit, they offer a slightly larger and more unique footprint than the standard rectangular rooms.

  • Two Extra Long Twin beds
  • Two desks with chairs
  • Two wardrobes

Bathrooms & Kitchens

Bathrooms here are communal but modern. Each floor has communal bathrooms with private, lockable stalls for both showers and toilets. There is also one single-use bathroom per floor for extra privacy.

Kitchens are located in each duplex lounge, meaning there is one kitchen available for every two floors.

Final Thoughts

If you want a quiet, isolated experience, Kelly is NOT for you. But if you want to make friends fast, it’s arguably the best spot on campus.

Pros: Brand new 2023 renovation, extremely social layout, and the massive Kelly Commons space.

Cons: Can be noisy due to the open duplex lounges, and some students report issues with temperature control.
