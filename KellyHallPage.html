The social rundown

Address

203 N. 34th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19104

Cost

About $4,015 per term

The Setup

10 floors connected by central spiral staircases that link "neighborhoods" of two floors each.

Building Status

Fully renovated in 2023. Everything inside is brand new.

The Kelly Vibe

Kelly Hall is basically the go-to spot if you're a social butterfly. After the massive 2023 renovation, it went from being one of the oldest-feeling dorms to arguably the most modern on campus. It has a reputation for being the easiest place to meet people, mostly because the building is designed to keep everyone out of their rooms and in the common areas.

The Commons: The new "Kelly Commons" addition is huge. It's the only place to study when your roommate is being too loud.
The Neighborhoods: Instead of long, lonely hallways, floors are grouped together via central staircases and double-height lounges.

Room Life

The rooms are traditional doubles with vinyl tile floors. If you can manage to snag a corner unit, they’re the hidden gems of the building—they have a slightly larger and more unique footprint than the standard rectangular rooms. Just be ready for the noise; because the lounges are so open, sound travels.

The Student Reality

If you want a quiet, isolated experience, Kelly is definitely not for you. But if you want to make friends fast, it’s the best spot on campus. Just a heads up: some floors have issues with the temperature control when the seasons change, and the open staircases mean you'll probably hear your neighbors a lot more than in Millennium.

Practical Details

  • Bathrooms: They’re communal but modern. You get private, lockable stalls for showers and toilets, so it doesn't feel like a locker room.
  • Kitchens: One kitchen for every two floors, located in the duplex lounges. They can get crowded on weekends.
  • Laundry: One dedicated room on the lower level. It’s a hike if you live on the 10th floor, so plan accordingly.

This guide is based on student life post-renovation, not the old brochures.
