The Specs

The basic high-rise

Address

101 N. 34th Street. Super central to the main campus buildings.

Cost

About $3,840 per term

The Setup

15 floors of traditional corridor layouts. Rooms line both sides of a long main hallway.

Who Lives Here

Only first-year students. It's loud, but you meet everyone fast.

The Towers Vibe

Towers is the definition of a standard freshman college dorm. If your main goal is to meet as many people as possible during welcome week, just stay here. The layout is identical on all 15 floors, and because the floor lounges are right by the elevators, they turn into the natural social hubs where everyone hangs out.

The Rooms: The actual bedrooms are surprisingly big for a traditional hallway dorm, so you won't feel like you're stepping on your roommate's toes.
The Bay Windows: Every room has a deep bay window. It lets in a ton of light and gives you a nice little nook to set up a seating area or hide extra storage bins.

Room Setup

The bedrooms are standard double-occupancy spaces measuring roughly 12' x 15'. You get vinyl tile floors in the rooms and standard carpet out in the corridors. Each space comes furnished with two extra-long twin beds, wardrobes, and desks with chairs. It's basic, but it works.

The Resident Reality

Towers is great for making friends, but the elevators are a nightmare during peak class times. If you live on floor 4 or below, just take the stairs or you'll be late to class. The communal bathrooms can get pretty rough on the weekends, and the whole building feels a bit dated compared to newly renovated spots like Bentley.

Practical Details

  • The Bathrooms: Traditional multi-stall setup down the hall with communal showers and toilets. Note that Gender-Inclusive Housing isn't an option in this building.
  • Kitchens: Available on floors 3 through 15, equipped with a freezer, stove, oven, and sink.
  • Lounges: Floor 2 has a quiet study space, Floor 1 has a large multipurpose room with a TV, and every residential floor has its own smaller lounge area.
  • Laundry: Centrally located in a dedicated laundry room up on Floor 2.
