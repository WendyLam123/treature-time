The Specs

Reliable living

Address

115 N. 32nd Street. Tucked away but pretty easy to get to everything.

Cost

About $4,310 per term

The Setup

7 floors of 4 and 6-person suites. 2 people per bedroom sharing a central living area and kitchenette.

Bike Storage

Indoor bike storage is right on the first floor, which saves you from hauling it downstairs.

The Caneris Vibe

Caneris Hall is basically the definition of solid and reliable. It doesn't have a wild reputation like some of the other halls, but it’s known for being clean, well-maintained, and straightforward. The common areas got a furniture and TV refresh a while back, and the building has a decent setup for entertainment if you want to hang out outside your room.

The Split Bathroom: Inside the suites, the bathroom space is split into completely separate shower and toilet rooms. It makes sharing with multiple roommates way less of a logistical headache in the morning.
Kitchenettes: Every suite has its own little kitchenette area with a sink, cabinet space, and an 18-cubic-foot refrigerator for your day-to-day snacks.

Room Layout & Furniture

The bedrooms are standard rectangles measuring about 11' x 16.4'. Because the layout is predictable, it’s actually pretty easy to arrange your furniture without playing weird spatial games. You get your standard Extra Long Twin beds, wardrobes, dressers, and desks that come with a chair. The floors are tile inside the suites and carpet out in the residential corridors.

The Resident Reality

Caneris is super clean and the free laundry is great, but some students find it a little plain or forgettable compared to the high-energy halls. The study rooms are on the smaller side, so if you need a massive, sprawling desk area to focus, you'll probably end up heading over to the library instead of staying on your floor.

Practical Details

  • The Bathrooms: 4-person suites get a separate shower room with a sink and a toilet room with a sink. 6-person suites have a shower room with two stalls and a toilet room with two toilets, with the sinks located outside both rooms.
  • Floor Kitchens: There is a full community kitchen on every single floor. The ones on floors 2–7 are massive with two stoves and two ovens, while the first floor has one stove and one oven.
  • Laundry Facilities: Super easy to find—the laundry rooms are located right next to the community kitchens and feature two washers and two dryers each.
  • Perks & Lounges: There is gaming equipment scattered throughout the building, and the first floor has a dedicated multipurpose room with a TV for movie nights.
