Are You Wondering What to Eat in Drexel?

Drexel's meal plans provide a flexible and convenient way to manage student meals. Students are required to purchase one meal plan during their freshman year. There are three options for freshmen and seven more options for upperclassmen and graduate students. Dining plans are mainly made up of two components: meal swipes and dining dollars.

  • Meal Swipes: 1 meal swipe = one all-you-care-to-eat meal at the dining hall (currently Urban Eatery only).
    • Students can also use one meal swipe to exchange a meal at the Perelman Center for Jewish Life Café.

  • Dining Dollars: a balance loaded into your dining account.
    • Use them to purchase food at Drexel campus dining locations that accept Dining Dollars (e.g., Northside Dining Terrace and Starbucks at Gerri C. LeBow Hall).
    • Not redeemable for cash.

Things You Should to Know:

  • Unused Dining Dollars transfer to the next term if students purchase the next term's dining plan before the deadline.
    • Check the deadline here! Click Here
  • Unused meal swipes do not transfer to the next term, so be cautious of unused meal swipes.
  • Guest Meal: Students can invite their friends or family (from outside or inside Drexel) to enjoy a meal with them.

Which Plan Should You Choose?

First Year Plans:

  • Not a breakfast person? Want a cheaper option?
    • Choose Weekly 14! You can have dinner and lunch every day.

  • Breakfast person? Athlete? Want more food choices?
    • Choose All Access. You can enjoy as many meals as you like.

Upperclassman and Graduate Students:

Upperclassmen and Graduate Students Have More Options!

You can choose from the three plans above (more meal swipes and Dining Dollars), or add Dining Dollars only, or a mix of meal swipes and Dining Dollars.

  • Stay on campus most of the time? Want a way to encourage regular eating habits?
    • Choose Weekly 14 + $250 Dining Dollars to build a healthy eating routine!

  • Want an easy way to budget meals but eat more flexibly?
    • Choose to add Dining Dollars per term to ensure you don't exceed your meal budget!

  • Miss the food at Urban Eatery, but also want more variety?
    • Choose Block 25 + $200 Dining Dollars so you can eat occasionally at Urban Eatery and also enjoy different food trucks and restaurants in Philly!
