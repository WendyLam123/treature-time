Introduction:

A meal plan provides a flexible and convenient way to manage student meals. But the more option student have, the more questions students may have regarding which meal plan to choose. To help students make a wise decision, here are all the meal plan options Drexel provides to freshmen and upperclassman and graduate students. Drexel freshmen have 3 options. Drexel upperclassmen and graduate students have 7 more options other than the 3 options they can choose.

First Year Plans:

Weekly 14 + 250 Dining

  • $2,210
  • Meal Swipes: 14 meals each week
  • Dining Dollars: $250 per term
  • Guest meal: 2

All Access + 225 Dining Dollars

  • $2,380
  • Meal Swipes: Unlimited
  • Dining Dollars: $225 per term
  • Guest meal: 2

All Access + 200 Dining Dollars

  • $2,565
  • Meal Swipes: Unlimited
  • Dining Dollars: $400 per term
  • Guest meal: 2

Important Information:

  • Unused Dining dollars will transfer to the next term if students have purchased the next term dining plan before the deadline. 
  • Unused meal swipes will not transfer to the next term, so be cautious of unused meal swipes.
  • Meal exchange: You can use 1 meal per meal period to exchange meals at the Perelman Center for Jewish Life Cafe.
  • All plans above allow meal exchange at The Perelman Center for Jewish Life.
  • All plans above have 2 meals for guests each term. Students can invite their friends or family outside or inside the school to enjoy the meal with them.

Which to choose?

  • Not a breakfast person? Choose Weekly 14! You can have dinner and lunch every day.
  • Breakfast person? Choose All Access, and you can access as many meals as you like.
