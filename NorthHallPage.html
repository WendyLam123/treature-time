The Specs

The apartment alternative

Address

3200 Race Street. Pretty convenient location right near the main academic buildings.

Cost

About $4,310 per term

The Setup

6 floors of 4 and 6-person suites. A central spiral staircase splits the building into east and west wings.

Bike Storage

Located down on the basement level if you need to lock your bike up inside.

The Suite Life Vibe

If you want to feel like you live in a real apartment instead of a classic, cramped dorm room, North Hall is your best option. It’s set up with 4 and 6-person suites that have their own living areas. Because of the layout, it stays way quieter and more relaxed than the traditional hallway high-rises.

The Full Kitchenette: Every suite has its own kitchen space with cabinets, a sink, and a full-sized refrigerator, which is huge for storing your own food.
6th Floor Lounge: Everyone's favorite study spot. It’s a massive room with wall-to-wall windows that look right out over the city skyline.

Room Setup

The bedrooms inside the suites are double-occupancy and measure about 11'9" x 16'2". The floors are tile inside the suites and standard carpet down the main building corridors. The rooms come furnished with your typical extra-long twin beds, wardrobes, and standard desks with chairs.

The Resident Reality

North Hall gives you a lot of space, but suite living means extra work. Since you have a private bathroom and living area, you and your roommates have to clean it all yourselves—which can easily turn into chore drama if you don't coordinate early. Also, the walls are notoriously thin, so you'll probably hear your suite-mates talking in the next room.

Practical Details

  • The Bathrooms: 4-person suites have a separate shower room with a sink, and a separate toilet room with a sink. 6-person suites get a shower room with two stalls, a bathroom with two toilets, and shared sinks outside.
  • Community Kitchens: Floors 2-5 have two shared kitchens with ovens and stoves for heavy cooking. Floors 1, 6, and the basement have one kitchen each.
  • Laundry: Unbelievably convenient—there is a dedicated laundry room located right on every single floor so you don't have to haul baskets down an elevator.
  • First Floor: Features a game room with gaming gear and a separate multipurpose room with a TV.
