The Specs

The social suite choice

Address

3300 Race Street. Incredible location if you want to be close to classes and the athletic center.

Cost

About $4,310 per term

The Setup

12 floors of 4-person apartment-style suites, with roughly 9 to 13 units on each residential floor. Halls are mixed gender and rooms are same gender.

Bike Storage

Indoor bike storage is tucked away on the basement level with DragonCard access.

The Race Street Vibe

Race Street Residences is widely known as the social or high-energy suite hall on campus. It’s designed like a massive collection of small apartments, featuring two bedrooms, a shared living area, and a kitchenette per unit. The building’s unique L-shaped architecture creates some cool, interesting angles in the common areas and gives you completely different views depending on which wing you land in.

The Double Shower: This is a massive logistical win for a 4-person group. Having two separate showers and bathrooms drastically cuts down on morning traffic when everyone is trying to rush out to class.
Location Perks: You are a 3 minute walk away from the athletic center and a 10 minute walk away from all the main campus buldings including One Drexel Plaza and the CCI building.

Room Setup & Dimensions

The bedrooms inside the suites measure 11' x 13'. The floors are fully tiled inside your suite area, switching over to carpet out in the residential corridors. The rooms come furnished with the standard extra-long twin beds, wardrobes, and desks with chairs, while the kitchenettes area includes a full-sized refrigerator, sink, and cabinet space. There are communual kitchens on each floor with another full sized fridge and two ovens.

The Resident Reality

Race Street gives you the perfect mix of apartment independence and a great social scene, but it can get pretty loud on weekends. It has a reputation for being way more of a "party hall" compared to the super quiet, studious vibe over at North Hall. If you’re a light sleeper who needs total silence to study, the noise levels here might frustrate you.

Practical Details

  • The Bathrooms: Features a full bathroom with a shower, alongside an additional shower room and a vanity sink located in the hallway.
  • Laundry Facilities: Unlike some suite halls with rooms on every level, the laundry facilities here are centrally located on Floor 1.
  • Floor Lounges: Floors 2 through 11 each feature a dedicated social lounge equipped with a TV, paired right next to a separate, smaller quiet study lounge. The second floor also features a faith room for students and a second floor lounge often used for studying with multiple seating options and a large TV you can connect your computer to.
  • Ground Floor: Floor 1 houses a full multipurpose room with a TV and pool table. There are also vending machines and a little library!

Based entirely on actual student feedback and campus life. Take official marketing brochures with a grain of salt.
