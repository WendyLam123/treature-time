Overview

Bentley Hall is widely considered one of the best traditional dorms because it was completely reimagined in 2019. It houses the Honors College, so it has a slightly more academic and quieter reputation compared to other freshman halls.

The building feels modern and clean, featuring unique "living room" gallery spaces and seminar rooms on the ground floor.

Building Layout

The layout emphasizes community by alternating between social and study lounges on every floor. This gives you a dedicated spot to either hang out or focus depending on what floor you're on.

Rooms

Rooms are standard double-occupancy, measuring approximately 12' x 16'. The furniture is modern, but keep in mind that the bed frames are at a set height and are not adjustable.

  • Two Extra Long Twin beds
  • Two desks (no storage/shelves) + chairs
  • Two-drawer dressers
  • Wardrobes

Bathrooms & Kitchens

Bentley uses a hallway-style bathroom setup, but they are individual-entry, meaning you still get privacy despite them being communal. Each floor has two gender-specific multi-stall bathrooms and two single-occupancy bathrooms.

Unlike some halls with kitchens on every floor, Bentley's primary community kitchen is located on the first floor.

Final Thoughts

Bentley is the "safe bet" for students who want a modern feel without the cramped cylindrical shape of Millennium.

Pros: Newest facilities, great mix of study/social space, and individual-entry bathrooms.

Cons: Can feel "too academic" for some, and you have to hike to the first floor to use the kitchen.
