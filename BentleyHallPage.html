The Bentley Vibe

Bentley is basically the "sophisticated" choice for freshman housing. Because it houses the Honors College, people assume it’s just a library with beds, but it’s actually one of the cleanest and most modern buildings on the block. It was completely reimagined in 2019, so you aren't dealing with that "old dorm" smell you find in other traditional halls.

The Lounges: Every floor alternates between social and study lounges. If your floor is too loud, just go up or down one level to find a quiet spot.
Privacy: Even though it's a "communal" bathroom setup, they are individual-entry. You get your own private space to shower without the awkward curtain vibes.

Room Life

The rooms are standard 12' x 16' doubles. The furniture is fresh and modern, but there is one big catch: the beds are at a fixed height. Don't bring those plastic bed risers from Target because you can't actually adjust the frames to loft them.

The Resident Reality

Bentley is definitely the "safe bet" if you want a modern room and care about your sleep. It’s way quieter than Kelly. The only real pain is the kitchen situation—there's only one, and it's on the first floor. If you live on floor 6 and want to make ramen, you’re going for a hike.

Practical Details

  • Bathrooms: Two gender-specific multi-stall bathrooms and two single-occupancy bathrooms per floor. They’re kept pretty clean compared to the high-rises.
  • Kitchen & Laundry: Both are stuck on the first floor. It gets crowded on Sunday nights, so try to do your laundry on a Tuesday morning if you can.
  • Furniture: You get the standard Twin XL bed, a wardrobe, a desk (heads up: no built-in shelves), and a dresser.
