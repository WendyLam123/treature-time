Welcome to Your New Home

Drexel University offers a variety of residence halls designed to fit different lifestyles. Some students prefer a traditional hallway layout, others prefer suite-style apartment living, and some want a unique hybrid experience. This is the unfiltered reality of campus living.

The "Reddit" Consensus: Unfiltered Student Advice

"If you choose Millennium, prepare for the 'Millennium Shake'. During high winds, the building sways slightly. It's totally safe, but it'll definitely wake you up at 2 AM if you aren't used to it."

"The Towers elevators are a nightmare during peak class times. If you live on floor 4 or below, just take the stairs. Your legs will thank you, and you'll actually make it to class on time."

Style Guide: Which one fits you?

Traditional Halls: Feature hallway corridor layouts with shared community spaces. These are the most social options for freshmen who want to make friends fast.

Suite Style: Provide shared living rooms and kitchenettes. You get more space, but you are responsible for cleaning your own bathroom!

Which Dorm Should You Choose?

🚀 The Socialite: Choose Towers or Kelly. You'll meet everyone in the first week.
📚 The Scholar: Choose Bentley. It’s quiet and literally seconds from the library.
🍳 The Chef: Choose Van Rensselaer. The only dorm with actual gas ranges.
🏙️ The View Seeker: Choose Millennium. The 17th-floor lounge is unbeatable.

Technical Standards

  • Furniture: All rooms come with a bed (Twin XL), desk, chair, and wardrobe per student.
  • AC: All halls except some older units in North feature full central air conditioning.
  • Connectivity: DragonFly Wi-Fi and Ethernet ports are standard in every room.
