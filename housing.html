Introduction:

Drexel University offers a variety of residence halls designed to fit different lifestyles. Some students prefer a traditional hallway layout, others prefer suite-style apartment living, and some want a unique hybrid experience. Below are all housing options available to first-year and upperclass students.

Traditional Style Halls:

Traditional halls feature hallway corridor layouts with shared community spaces. These buildings are often the most social.

Bentley Hall

Style: Traditional

Renovated in 2019

Alternating study & social lounges

Honors College community

Private-entry shared bathrooms

Kelly Hall

Style: Traditional

Renovated in 2023

Spiral staircase “neighborhood” design

Very social environment

Towers Hall

Style: Traditional

15-floor high-rise

Deep bay windows in every room

Classic freshman dorm experience

Suite Style Halls:

Suite-style halls provide shared living rooms and kitchenettes, offering a more apartment-like experience.

North Hall

4–6 person suites

Full kitchenettes

Massive 6th floor study lounge

Quieter, apartment-style feel

Race Street Residences

4-person apartment-style suites

Two showers per unit

Very social / party reputation

Close to classes and gym

Caneris Hall

4–6 person suites

Private shared bathrooms

Free laundry

Clean and reliable

Van Rensselaer Hall

3–4 person historic suites

Gas ranges in kitchenettes

Free laundry

Independent living feel

Hybrid Style Hall:

Hybrid halls combine traditional and suite features with unique architecture.

Millennium Hall

17-story cylindrical tower

Multi-purpose videogame room

Best skyline views

Very social engineering community

Important Information:

  • Traditional halls typically have shared hallway bathrooms.
  • Suite halls require students to clean their own shared bathrooms.
  • Race Street and North are popular for apartment-style living.
  • Millennium offers private bathrooms but smaller room sizes.
  • Van Rensselaer is best for independent students who like to cook.

Which Dorm Should You Choose?

  • Want the most social experience? Choose Towers or Kelly.
  • Want the newest/most modern? Choose Bentley or Kelly.
  • Want as many roommates as possible? Choose North.
  • Want a suite and social vibe? Choose Race Street.
  • Want privacy and skyline views? Choose Millennium.
