Customers' reviews from Google reviews:

  • Location & convenience: Urban Eatery is generally described as convenient for students due to its campus proximity and walkable location.
  • Cleanliness & maintenance: Feedback is mixed. Some reviewers note that dining areas and restrooms are kept clean and well-maintained, while others mention occasional concerns about overall cleanliness during busy periods.
  • Food quality & variety: Opinions vary significantly. Many students appreciate the variety of food stations and options available, while others feel that food quality is inconsistent depending on the station or time of day.
  • Crowding & experience: A common theme is that the dining hall can become crowded during peak meal times, which may affect the overall dining experience.
